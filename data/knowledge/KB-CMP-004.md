# Evidence-rich multi-system handoff / 多系统问题证据交接

## Symptoms / 症状
unknown multi-system root cause; 多系统原因不明

## Clarify / 澄清
Ask business impact, urgency and what has already been tried.

## Safe investigation / 安全排查
Collect service snapshots, changes and relevant run identifiers without secrets. Separate observed facts from hypotheses, preserve failures and unknowns. Do not fabricate a root cause or ETA.

## Verification / 验证
Generating handoff does not resolve the underlying issue.

## Handoff / 交接
Assign suggested Data Platform team and open questions; explicitly state no real ticket has been sent.
