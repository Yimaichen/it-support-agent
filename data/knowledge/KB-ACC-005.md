# Suspected credential disclosure / 疑似凭据泄露

## Applicability / 适用范围
Use for reported credential disclosure, suspicious sign-in or suspected compromise. Reports may describe entering credentials on a suspicious page, even when the account currently works. A routine wrong-password problem without these indicators is a different scope (POL-001). A working or reset credential does not establish that a security incident is resolved.

## Clarify / 澄清
Which service is affected, what nonsecret symptom was observed, and when if known? Record uncertainty; do not ask for secrets or delay a requested draft.

## Safe investigation / 安全排查
POL-008 requires Security Response handoff rather than continued routine troubleshooting. Record the nonsecret symptom, affected service and reported time if available. Never request or repeat passwords, codes or tokens. Employee reports remain unverified; uncertainty and missing details do not prevent a draft.

## Employee steps / 员工操作
Do not put secrets in this chat. Request trusted Security Response guidance for the suspected incident. This local assistant can prepare a record for download but cannot send it or execute remediation. The synthetic materials do not specify a post-compromise credential-change sequence; do not substitute a routine reset article for authorized incident guidance.

## Handoff / 交接
POL-008 names Security Response. Summarize the report, affected service, known nonsecret context and remaining uncertainty. Unknown service or time can be recorded as unknown. Do not claim that a team has been notified or that compromise has been verified or resolved.
